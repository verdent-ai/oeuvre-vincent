# OEUVRE — Vincent: A Life in Ten Frames

An interactive scroll through the brief, brilliant life of Vincent van Gogh, told in ten paintings.

🔗 **[Try it live](https://verdent-ai.github.io/oeuvre-vincent/)**

---

## About

In thirty-seven years, Vincent van Gogh painted nearly nine hundred canvases. He sold one.

This immersive web experience guides you through ten pivotal paintings that trace his journey — from the dark peasant scenes of Nuenen to the swirling starry nights of Saint-Rémy, ending with the wheatfields of Auvers-sur-Oise.

## The Ten Frames

1. **The Potato Eaters** (1885, Nuenen)
2. **Self-Portrait with a Straw Hat** (1887, Paris)
3. **The Yellow House** (1888, Arles)
4. **The Bedroom** (1888, Arles)
5. **Café Terrace at Night** (1888, Arles)
6. **Sunflowers** (1888, Arles)
7. **Self-Portrait with Bandaged Ear** (1889, Arles)
8. **The Starry Night** (1889, Saint-Rémy)
9. **Almond Blossom** (1890, Saint-Rémy)
10. **Wheatfield with Crows** (1890, Auvers)

## How to Experience

Simply scroll horizontally to walk through the gallery. Each painting is accompanied by the story behind it — told in Vincent's own words through his letters to his brother Theo.
